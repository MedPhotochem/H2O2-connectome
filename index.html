What this network represents

A link A → B means that A cites B according to OpenAlex-indexed references. For a selected paper, blue arrows enter it (papers citing it), while orange arrows leave it (papers it cites). Links do not establish collaboration, thematic similarity or intellectual influence.

The absence of an edge does not establish the absence of a citation: older publications may lack DOIs, be absent from the index or have incomplete digitized bibliographies.

The network starts with 75 fixed references. You may add DOI records (yellow squares) and optionally fetch one selected paper’s direct citing articles (pale-blue nodes). Only the selected publication is expanded, with up to 300 retrieved citing records; all other references remain unexpanded.
